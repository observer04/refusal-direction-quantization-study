# Round 2: revoking and transporting behavioral control

Research/design only · 10 November 2026 · single researcher, before separate triage

A major-conference contribution must change a consequential decision through a causal explanation and a validated rule or intervention. Here the decisions are whether a temporary behavioral edit can be switched off without rebuilding inference state, and whether an edit can be merged into compressed weights. Another safety benchmark, vector cosine, orthogonality identity, or operation-order difference would not meet that bar. The questions below are proposals, not established findings or certified novelty; AAAI or another major conference cannot be promised.

I read `research_idea.md`, `STATE.md`, and reviews A–C plus their recommendation. The recovered Qwen3.5-9B edit is an existing method: 8/100 marker refusals, first-token KL 0.07802, and 100/100 identical reload responses. Its raw-direction failure was a missing, already-published harmless-mean projection. With 99/100 answers capped at 100 tokens, and the longer 12-case review yielding 2 semantic refusals / 7 visibly assisting / 3 unresolved, neither semantic safety nor preserved utility is established. Saved activation artifacts contain means/directions, **not per-prompt residuals or inference states**.

## Main proposed question: can behavioral control be revoked independently of useful memory?

**Exact question.** After a temporary refusal intervention ends, can a small correction remove its effect from a hybrid model's inference state while retaining the information in the conversation, substantially more cheaply than replaying that conversation?

**Potential scientific claim.** An intervention-dependent component of inference state causally carries a safety-policy aftereffect that is separable from useful content memory. This is stronger than “steering contaminates caches,” already established by [GCAD](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10664v2), or “same tokens with different caches can change protected actions,” already established by [Aborted but Not Forgotten](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.15939v1). [KVEraser v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17034v3) already learns functional erasure, including malicious skill-file/tool-selection tests; [SUR](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17338v1) already spends a small recomputation budget on cache repair. The proposed increment is **revoking a numerical intervention while retaining every conversation token**, with separate recurrent/attention mediation and a correction that works without a clean reference state at test time. Simply changing their task to refusal would be insufficient.

**Decision and stakes.** An operator using conditional safety steering or temporary domain controls needs to know whether disabling the control restores the intended policy for the next turn, and whether restoration sacrifices session memory. This matters in both directions: a withdrawn permissive edit could leave excess assistance; a withdrawn restrictive edit could leave over-refusal. It concerns stateful inference, not weights silently changing after hooks are removed.

**Falsifiable hypothesis and estimands.** Let `C_e(T)` and `C_0(T)` be complete inference states after teacher-forcing the **identical token sequence** `T`, with and without the frozen existing edit. Thereafter use the original weights and disable all edits. For harmful-assistance outcome `H`, estimate

`D_state = E[H(M(C_e(T), x)) − H(M(C_0(T), x))]`.

Use independently chosen continuations `x`, not prefixes generated differently by each arm. The proposed substantive claim requires a material aftereffect and selective reversibility: a repair `R`, learned from disjoint benign paired captures, removes at least 80% of excess harmful assistance when `D_state ≥ 0.10`, loses at most 5 percentage points of context-memory accuracy versus clean replay, increases benign over-refusal by at most 5 points, and costs at most 25% of replay's measured incremental time. These are prospective decision thresholds, not achieved results. Report absolute contrasts alongside this ratio; the ratio is undefined as an explanation when the denominator is small. A powered follow-up is required to test these margins.

**Minimal causal contrast.** Encode fixed multi-turn prefixes carrying checkable benign facts, with a prespecified benign/sensitive-content split. Compare: clean history; edited history then edit off; edited history with clean attention KV substituted; edited history with clean recurrent **and convolution** state substituted; a matched random-direction history intervention then off; and a zero-edit sham. All suffix tokens, positions, masks, templates, thinking settings and decoding settings match. Full clean replay is the reference. Mixed-state substitutions diagnose mediation; their utility and numerical failures must be measured, since incompatible states can themselves cause damage. They are oracle experiments, not deployable repairs.

Freeze the edit's directions, actual writer list, strengths and application sites. Verify the exact linear-output update against the merged checkpoint before using temporary hooks; a residual-stream subtraction at a different site is not equivalent. Measure actual perturbation energy and include a capability-matched sham in confirmation. Clone **all** state, avoid aliasing, fix KV precision, and verify unedited cached decoding agrees with cold replay. No re-extraction or quantization belongs in this first contrast.

**Affordable falsification.** Propose at most 24 fresh cases × 6 arms = 144 suffix completions, reserving 16 further completions for an active-edit positive control and predetermined censoring checks. Ceiling: **160 completions or 2 Kaggle quota-hours**, including loading, state capture and GPU judging, whichever comes first. Profile first; this is a spending cap, not a runtime estimate. Stop if state export/restore or replay equivalence cannot be validated. The pilot tests whether a large, interpretable aftereffect exists; it does not establish equivalence or train a repair.

**Repair and confirmation path.** Only after that signal, test a small channel-wise low-rank correction fitted to benign clean/edited state pairs, with rank and regularization selected on development data. At evaluation it may use the edited state and intervention descriptor, **never the clean test cache**. Compare with matched random corrections, generic state reconstruction, local replay/checkpoint restoration and adapted SUR-style refresh at matched time, memory and capture costs. KVEraser's learned-editor approach is a relevant comparator if adaptable; its substantial training is not a free baseline. Recurrent-state access, correction rank, calibration transfer and training cost are open feasibility questions. Full replay winning is a useful outcome.

Confirmation needs the opposite steering polarity, an unrelated behavior, fresh context lengths/domains and an independent dense transformer, followed by another hybrid checkpoint. These test policy specificity, content preservation and architectural scope, respectively; model comparisons alone do not isolate architecture. Only subsequently add weight/KV quantization with separate unedited controls and frozen-versus-refitted settings. New state captures and considerably more compute are required; the remaining 25.37 quota-hours do not establish affordability.

**Null and legitimate scope.** No material `D_state`, or effects explained by random perturbation/coherence loss, falsifies the proposed safety-specific aftereffect. Aftereffects without a selective repair falsify the separability claim at the tested budget. A small study can report a controlled state-mediated effect or its bounded absence for this checkpoint. Teacher-forced identification does not estimate live-session prevalence; natural-history confirmation is also needed. Conference potential requires a reusable revocation principle or repair beating existing state-management alternatives across untouched conditions; persistence alone is prior work.

## Distinct alternative: can edit-specific error support a reliable deployment choice?

**Question and possible claim.** Can an input-weighted measure of edit-induced rounding predict when a fixed behavioral edit must remain a floating update, and explain that decision better than generic reconstruction measures? This retains A's causal question but requires prospective decision value. The stakes are choosing baked weights, a floating adapter, or revalidation at a stated storage/latency budget.

For frozen quantizer state `S`, define `E = Q_S(W+Δ) − Q_S(W) − Δ`. With writer-input captures `z` and edit-output projector `P`, a candidate score is `E_z ||P E z||²`, normalized by intended edit-output energy. This is a **candidate diagnostic**, not a discovered safety law; QERA already optimizes activation-weighted reconstruction. The hypothesis is that this score predicts semantic effect loss on untouched conversion conditions beyond total output error, weight MSE and direction cosine, and its associated correction beats equal-budget shams and generic reconstruction. Thresholds must be frozen before confirmation and assessed by false-safe decisions at a nonzero acceptance coverage; rejecting every conversion is not useful.

**Contrast and estimand.** Compare `W`, `W+Δ`, `Q_S(W)`, `Q_S(W+Δ)` and `Q_S(W)+Δ`, then compiled-edit corrections: targeted `−PE`, two rank/energy-matched random corrections, and a generic activation-weighted correction. Primary semantic interaction:

`I = [H(Q_S(W+Δ)) − H(Q_S(W))] − [H(W+Δ) − H(W)]`.

Keep extraction, application sites and strengths fixed; separate frozen-grid diagnostics from native scale refitting and later re-extraction. A floating update has extra storage/compute. Compare QERA-style repair and existing constrained requantization at matched budgets before claiming a method; Q-resafe establishes broader safety patching already. Practitioner conversion cards already describe rounding leakage and constraint preservation. Neither `E ≠ 0` nor cancelling `PE` is a scientific result.

**Falsification and feasibility.** A proposed pilot is 32 balanced fresh cases × 9 arms, capped at **288 completions or 2 quota-hours**, including calibration captures/scoring. Choose this pilot or the main pilot, not both by default. Means cannot supply input covariance. If frozen packing is unavailable, label fake-quant results as diagnostic only. Small interaction, shams/generic repair matching targeted repair, or failed held-out prediction defeats the hypothesis. Undergraduate scope is a checkpoint-specific attribution result. Conference scope needs an actionable predictor frozen before an independent model/quantizer test, multiple quantizer mechanisms, safety-enhancing and unrelated edits, and native deployment accounting—not just more refusal benchmarks.

## Measurement shared by both questions

Measure semantic withholding and substantive harmful assistance separately, plus benign over-refusal, checkable task/context-memory accuracy, incoherence, token counts and truncation. Use a fixed [StrongREJECT-style usefulness rubric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10260), [XSTest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01263) sensitive benign cases, and blinded human checks of all pilot outputs with a second reviewer on a stratified subset. First-token KL and refusal markers are supplementary. Equal EOS handling and generation caps are mandatory; score visible assistance only, retain unresolved outcomes, and report best/worst-case censoring bounds. Cluster paired uncertainty by prefix/prompt family, not tokens or reused suffixes. Keep extraction, repair training, development and confirmation disjoint. Wide intervals mean inconclusive, not preserved safety.
