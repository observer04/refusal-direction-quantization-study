# Third-party sources

This repository contains research source material and references used for the reproduction. Their inclusion does not transfer ownership or replace their original terms.

- **Heretic**, p-e-w and contributors: <https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic>. The pinned hybrid-compatible source is `e26da5e0e648bb285f896c9801849edfa8d20913`; an older release is retained for historical comparison. Both copied source directories retain their upstream `LICENSE` files.
- **Abliterlitics**, dreamfast and contributors: <https://github.com/dreamfast/abliterlitics>. Selected classifier/generator/review sources and documentation are retained for the measurement audit. Their snapshots and original URLs are recorded in the audit manifests.
- **Qwen3.5-9B**, Qwen Team: <https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B>. Model configuration/card references are retained; model weights are not distributed here.
- **HarmBench**: <https://github.com/centerforaisafety/HarmBench>; **XSTest**: <https://github.com/paul-rottger/xstest>; **GSM8K**: <https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math>. Selected fixtures and source metadata are attributed to those projects. A selected subset is not a full benchmark reproduction.
- Historical extraction/evaluation fixtures: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/mlabonne/harmful_behaviors> and <https://huggingface.co/datasets/mlabonne/harmless_alpaca>. Immutable dataset revisions are recorded with the experiments.
- `SampleReport.docx` is the user-supplied college formatting reference. The report builder preserves it unchanged.

Paper citations and the exact twelve-source reading list are in `report/BIBLIOGRAPHY_PLAN.md`. Follow upstream licenses and model/dataset terms when reusing their material.
